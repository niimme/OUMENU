Permanent Daily Stations

🏛️ Residential Colleges Everyday Classics

Available daily alongside the Dunham Hot Line & Headington Specialty Bars:

  • Breakfast Specialty Station: Made-to-order omelets, custom breakfast sandwiches, and Belgian waffles (M–F 8:00 AM – 10:30 AM).
  • The Grill: Fresh made-to-order burgers, chicken sandwiches, and fries.
  • Pizza by the slice: Fresh baked artisan pizzas.
  • Salad Bar: Wide selection of greens, fresh toppings, and house dressings.
  • Pasta Bar: Fresh pasta and assorted sauces.
  • Stir-Fry & Deli Bar: Custom wraps, sandwiches, and wok creations.
  • Desserts & Ice Cream: Assorted cakes, pies, cookies, and soft-serve ice cream machine.

🍽️ Couch Restaurants Daily Specialties

All-you-care-to-eat stations open daily:

  • The Breakfast Club: Hot scrambled eggs, biscuits with sausage gravy, bacon, sausage, hash browns, and made-to-order waffles (M–F 7:00 AM – 10:30 AM).
  • Chick-fil-A: The only all-you-care-to-eat Chick-fil-A in the world! Sandwiches, nuggets, and waffle fries.
  • Athens Café: Beef & chicken gyros, falafel, hummus, and Mediterranean favorites.
  • Casa Del Sol: Made-to-order burritos, tacos, quesadillas, nachos, and enchiladas.
  • Sooner Smokehouse: Gourmet made-to-order hamburgers, BBQ, and hearty sides.
  • La Roma Pizza & Pasta: Fresh brick-oven pizza and build-your-own pasta bake (Gluten-free and Halal options upon request).
  • Vegetation Station: Dedicated vegan and vegetarian specialty bar.
  • The Crimson Creamery & Sweet Shoppe: Frozen yogurt with toppings, assorted cookies, and Starbucks coffee.

🏆 Wagner Dining Hall (Headington Hall)

Located inside Headington Hall (not Headington College) • Scratch-made athletic and student performance dining:

  • Breakfast Kitchen: Made-to-order eggs, made-to-order waffles, rotating breakfast scrambles, pancakes, and oatmeal (M–F 7:00 AM – 10:30 AM).
  • Made-to-Order Line: Custom stir-fry, grill specialties, hot pasta, and savory curry.
  • Make-Your-Own Bar: Fresh deli sandwich counter and high-protein salad bar.
