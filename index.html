Residential Colleges

Headington & Dunham Colleges

Features Dunham’s daily hot scratch entrees, vegan specials, and Headington’s rotating specialty bars (Wing Bar, Breakfast Bar, Mac & Cheese Bar, Seafood Bar) along with everyday made-to-order grill, pizza, salad, and pasta stations.

Couch Restaurants

All-You-Care-To-Eat Center

The flagship OU dining center home to the world's only all-you-care-to-eat Chick-fil-A, wok-cooked Shanghai Stir-Fry, Chef’s Choice entrees, Athens Café (gyros & falafel), Casa Del Sol (burritos & tacos), Sooner Smokehouse BBQ, La Roma Pizza, and The Crimson Creamery.

Wagner Dining Hall

Inside Headington Hall (Athletic & Campus Dining)

Located inside Headington Hall (athletics & student residence – not to be confused with Headington College). Focuses on fresh, high-quality, nutrient-dense scratch cooking with rotating hot entrees, daily breakfast scrambles, whole grains, Lawry's smashed potatoes, fresh vegetables, custom Made-to-Order stir-fry, pasta, curry, and grill lines, plus full salad and deli bars.
